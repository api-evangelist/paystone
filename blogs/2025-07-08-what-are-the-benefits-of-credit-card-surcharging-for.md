Credit card processing fees have become one of the quietest (and most costly) line items for business owners today. Whether you’re running a local café, a national franchise, or a service-based business, a percentage of every credit card sale goes straight to the card networks and processors. Over time, those costs add up—often cutting directly into your profit margin.
